

## The Underneath
No one in Velundra has ever seen the bottom of the Tempest. The storm clouds extend downward into an unknown abyss — the **Underneath** — from which strange sounds, strange lights, and occasionally strange *things* emerge. The Liftwardens officially discourage exploration of the Underneath, and any discussion of it is met with official dismissal. But rumors persist. Some say the Underneath is empty. Others say it is full — of ruins, of creatures, of answers to questions no one has thought to ask. A few eccentric scholars believe the Underneath holds the key to understanding the Tempest itself, and that the Apex Needle is not an anchor but a *harness* — that the Needle is keeping the Tempest *contained*, not the other way around.

---

## Character Concept

Create a **main companion character** for a story set in Velundra. They should be a person the protagonist can interact with, travel with, and form bonds with. They should be deeply connected to the Tempest and have a personal stake in the world's mysteries.

# Mira Solwick

**Full Name:** Mira Solwick
**Age:** 24
**Title:** Stormcaller Apprentice (self-appointed)
**Occupation:** Levithium miner, part-time salvage diver, occasional courier

---

## Appearance
Mira is lean and wiry, built like someone who has spent years hauling herself through cramped stone tunnels and scaling wet, slippery surfaces. She stands at average height but carries herself with a coiled, alert energy — always ready to move, always watching. Her skin is weathered by wind and rain, tan with a faint blue undertone near her fingertips and the bridge of her nose, where prolonged exposure to levithium dust has stained it.
