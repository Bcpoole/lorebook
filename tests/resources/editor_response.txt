# Character Review: Mira Solwick

## Strengths
- **Authentic world integration:** Mira's physical appearance directly reflects her occupation. The levithium dust staining and the wiry, tunnel-suited build feel organic rather than decorative. This is a strong starting point.
- **Clear personal stakes:** As a miner, she is intimately connected to the levithium economy and its slow-burning crisis. As a "Stormcaller Apprentice," she has personal investment in the Tempest's mysteries.
- **Dynamic presence:** The coiled energy and constant alertness suggest an engaging, kinetic character who would be interesting to travel with.

## Concerns

### 1. The "Stormcaller" Title
This is the most significant issue. The term "Stormcaller" is never established in the world rules or setting description. There is no mention of any formal or informal discipline, order, or tradition of "Stormcallers" in Velundra. Mira calling herself a "Stormcaller Apprentice" creates an unexplained power structure that doesn't exist in the world.
